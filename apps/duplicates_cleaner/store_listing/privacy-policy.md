# Duplicate Sweep Privacy Policy

_Last updated: <date>_

Duplicate Sweep ("the app") is made by `<Your name or company>` ("we").
This policy explains what the app does with information on your device.

## The short version

The app does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. Everything
it does happens on your device.

## Files on your device

To find duplicates, the app reads the files in the folders and storage
locations you choose, and computes a fingerprint of their content. Files are
deleted only when you select them and confirm. File names, paths, contents
and fingerprints are processed on your device only and are never sent to us
or to anyone else.

On Android the app asks for "All files access" so it can scan and delete files
across your device storage. On iOS it can only reach its own folder in the
Files app and the folders you pick.

## Settings and log

The app saves your settings (for example scan type and size filters) and a
plain-text log of scans and deletions on your device. The log leaves your
device only if you tap Export Log and choose where to send it.

## Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google
(Google Play). We do not receive your payment details. The app only asks the
store whether you own Prime.

## Analytics, ads and tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and does not
track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from
anyone.
